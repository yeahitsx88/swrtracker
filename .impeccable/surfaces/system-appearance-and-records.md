# System appearance and records

## Purpose
Operate: every authenticated role can scan, filter, select and act on authorized records; choose a personal display mode. Central IT owns tenant branding and guarded project recommissioning.

## Established world
Preserve Axiom identity, Roboto, restrained square action buttons, 12px panels and the existing blue accent. Extend shared tokens to light/dark modes; tenant branding overrides only validated brand colors.

## Composition
Use semantic tables for record collections. Keep navigation, workflow steps, charts and explanatory prose in their native forms. Place filtering and selection actions before the table. Keep secondary management sections collapsible; active commands remain visible and locked.

## Interaction
Selection has a useful action: export authorized displayed records, or an existing guarded command. Server-paged inventories explicitly scope local sorting/filtering/export to the loaded page. No bulk action grants new authority. Personal appearance and tenant branding are separate controls.

## States and recovery
Readable light/dark empty, loading, disabled, error and focus states. Persist settings through authenticated APIs. Preserve exact command bodies and keys after uncertain responses; stale evidence requires reload. Recommission preparation preserves historical records and requires a fresh readiness review before reopening.

## Evidence and boundaries
Inspect role surfaces at desktop/mobile in both modes. No live customer data. Document migrations, authorization boundaries, approved lifecycle changes and unfinished coverage honestly.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Keep authorized operational records easy to scan, narrow and act on; make personal display preferences independent of tenant branding.

OWN-WORLD: Incumbent Axiom/Roboto operational UI, light slate and white panels, now an explicitly approved dark palette (#111820 ground, #19242e panels). Preserve existing brand artwork and danger semantics.

STORY: Authorized inventory → filter/sort → select → useful export or an existing guarded action. Recommissioning uses archived evidence → preparation → explicit current readiness review → recorded reopening.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Current identity and project navigation precede the working panel; filtering and selection actions precede record rows. Appearance names display mode first, followed by the separately authorized branding section.

FORM: User-specified incumbent extension; no concept seed or comp tournament required. Native fields, semantic tables, square buttons, compact collapsible sections. Preserve exact uncertain retries and deliberate reload after stale evidence.
