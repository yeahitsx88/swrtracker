### Measured Diagnostic Limits

The last-24-hour error proportion comes from recorded authorized project observations. Response timing compares the last hour with the preceding 23 hours, reports real sample counts and p95, and says “No observations” when a population is empty. A delay comparison appears only with at least ten samples in both periods and a measured current average over twice the prior average. These are endpoint processing measurements, including scope verification and excluding telemetry persistence, browser and network time; they are not a service-level promise. Submission-notification time measures recorded queueing to dispatch or local capture, not a person's receipt/read time or browser-click-to-survey-team latency.

Observations start with this version: no historical backfill or invented baseline. Invalid/revoked sessions, unauthorized foreign-project attempts, collection routes without verified scope and failed telemetry writes are excluded. Bodies, credentials, cookies, files, raw exception messages and SQL are not recorded. Prior request state is limited workflow context, not screen/form replay or a distributed trace. Best-effort recording can miss observations; absence is not proof that errors did not occur. Removed memberships, delayed requests, pending reviews and tenant reviews are separate counts, not an overall health score.
