## Spec decisions and explicit exclusions

- **Draft retention conflict:** §20 calls itself definitive and requires hard deletion at 30 days; §5 says drafts are “soft-deleted, never hard-deleted.” Implementing §20 requires an explicit audit-retention design because `ticket_events` is append-only and references tickets.
- **Numbering conflict:** §§5, 18, and 20 require a number only when a draft is submitted; §6 says it is assigned on every creation. The Phase 2 closeout bullet and definitive draft section favor submission-time numbering for Variant 1; the project-scoped sequence policy remains.
- **Direct-assignment status conflict:** §6’s complete enum has no `CREATED`, while the Variant 2 diagram uses it and current code persists it. A canonical status/migration choice is needed before enum retirement.
- **Scope boundaries:** Phase 3 owns the mobile UI/public request form and `companies.status`/`project_companies` migration (§§5, 18). Phase 4 owns reporting and audit surfaces, including CSV and historical resubmission-chain resolution (§§18, 21, 23). SSO, configurable rules, Redis/queues and external integrations are explicitly deferred by §15. Do not treat these as Phase 2 blockers solely because their future schema or copy appears in §§20–24.
