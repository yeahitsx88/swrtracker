## Independent review and limits

Independent review covered the new implementation and the approved phase5 commit range, with separate desktop/mobile capture review. Material corrections are recorded in the [completion audit](phase5-scoped-offboarding-completion-20261002.md). The finish verdict scores the named findings; it is not a claim that every future production risk has been eliminated.

External delivery remains at least once: a provider may deliver before a process loses the database commit. Stable outbox identity is passed to transport; the durable review queue is the authoritative work item. Fake transport tests prove retry/lease behavior without asserting provider exactly-once delivery.

Application barriers do not constrain unrestricted database-owner SQL. Operational SQL affecting lifecycle state must follow the documented tenant-first lock protocol. Pilot transport, trusted ingress, production security headers/topology, scanning/quotas, retention, accessibility certification, backup RPO/RTO, penetration testing, MFA/SSO and RLS remain explicit later owner/rollout decisions.

No remote CI result or deployment is inferred from local evidence.
