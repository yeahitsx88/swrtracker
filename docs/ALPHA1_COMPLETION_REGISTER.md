- **Approved incomplete** means an approved capability has an identified missing implementation; do not use this label merely for missing evidence.
- **Decision-blocked** means two materially different outcomes require an unresolved product decision. Only that path stops.
- **Superseded** means later authority explicitly replaces the old expectation.
